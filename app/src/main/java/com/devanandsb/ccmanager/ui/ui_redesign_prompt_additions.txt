### SCREEN 3: ADD/EDIT CARD (EditScreen)
A detailed data-entry screen with a live, 3D-effect miniature card preview.

**1. Live Miniature Preview (Top of screen)**
- **Box Dimensions:** Full width, 112dp height, 22dp rounded corners.
- **Style:** Frosted, glassmorphic container with a heavy drop shadow (14dp elevation). It displays the currently selected Bank/Network colors and logos dynamically as the user types.
- Tapping this preview opens a bottom sheet to customize the "Design" (card art).

**2. Quick Input Actions**
- Below the preview, two primary outlined tiles side-by-side:
  - "Scan card" (Camera icon)
  - "Tap card (NFC)" (Contactless icon)

**3. Data Entry Panels**
- Grouped into "Panels" (surface color, rounded corners) with section headers (e.g., "1. Card details", "2. Statement & Due date").
- **Inputs:** Uses standard Material 3 OutlinedTextFields.
- **Card Number Field:** Auto-formats with spaces. A Network Logo (Visa, MC) appears dynamically inside the trailing edge as soon as the BIN is recognized.

### MODAL: SCAN CARD OVERLAY (Camera / ML Kit)
A dark, immersive camera UI that mimics premium wallet apps (like Apple Pay or Google Wallet) when scanning a card.

**1. Camera Viewport & Controls**
- A full-screen camera preview (`AndroidView`).
- **Top Left:** A circular frosted close button.
- **Top Right (if Flash available):** A circular frosted flash toggle button.

**2. Scanning Reticle (The "Lock-On" Frame)**
- Placed in the center of the screen, adhering to a standard credit card aspect ratio (CARD_RATIO ~1.58).
- Highly rounded corners (22dp).
- **Idle State:** A white, semi-transparent border (25% opacity) with a pulsing "laser" line sweeping up and down inside the frame to indicate scanning.
- **Locking State:** When ML Kit detects a number, the frame turns Green (Good) and opacity increases to 55%.
- **Custom Drawn Brackets:** Four custom-drawn corner brackets frame the card, independent of the border.

**3. Scan Success Animation (The "Paytm/GPay" burst)**
- When successful, the camera view dissolves into a solid background.
- A bold "Verified/Check" animation plays: an expanding concentric emerald ripple.
- A 3D rendered card (the scanned result) springs into view from a tilted angle, performs a fast metallic sheen/gloss sweep across its surface, and settles completely flat.

**3. Vault Security Modal (Triggered by tapping the logo on Home)**
- A luxury dark-mode modal showcasing the "Air-Gapped Silicon Fortress" concept.
- **Center Graphic:** An animated pulsing ring around a 3D-styled fingerprint core.
- **Pillars:** Below the intro text, dark rounded rectangles (Color 0xFF161B36) detail the security features ("Completely Offline", "Locked with Your Fingerprint"). Each has a glowing tinted icon on the left.
